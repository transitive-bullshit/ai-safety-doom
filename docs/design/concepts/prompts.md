# Initial concept image prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-09-05. These are exploratory boards, not final game assets. The images were generated before the user clarified that the level should have normal Doom length; the pictured Sam encounter is therefore a later-level study, not the opening encounter. All depicted numbers, text, layout, and character likenesses are provisional.

## A — doom-faithful

```text
Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: exploratory visual direction board for a desktop-browser AI-safety parody of classic 1993 DOOM, not a finished asset or implementation screenshot.
Primary request: a convincing old-school Doom total-conversion game aesthetic, grounded in a real 2.5D Doom engine. Crisp enlarged low-resolution pixel art, limited 256-color-looking palette, chunky sprite enemies, flat floors, angular sector architecture, no modern 3D lighting, no smooth vector UI, no browser device frame. Audience: AI engineers, alignment researchers, nerdy tech Twitter.
Composition: landscape direction board with one dominant 4:3 gameplay viewport including an original-Doom-style stone status bar, plus a smaller title/menu study and three worried HUD face expression studies. Show actual playable-looking gameplay, not cinematic illustration. Clear separation between studies on a near-black background.
Scene: recognizable visual echo of Doom E1M1 opening techbase, with low stairs, angular wall recesses, a green armor-like pickup, and a glimpse of toxic courtyard. AI-risk demon sprite in mid-distance and a chunky first-person weapon at bottom center. The little central HUD portrait provisionally depicts a recognizable pixel-art Eliezer Yudkowsky caricature with increasing worry across the expression studies. No graphic wounds on faces.
Candidate naming: title logo "P(DOOM)" in monumental classic Doom-like pixel lettering. Menu study uses only "NEW RUN", "CHOOSE YOUR P(DOOM)", "1%", "10%", "50%", "99%". HUD labels only "AMMO", "HEALTH", "ARMOR". Keep text sparse and legible.
Constraints: affectionate fictional parody. Do not present a real human in photographic violence. All humans are small cartoon pixel sprites or HUD portraits. No Bloom/light mode in these initial boards. Do not add modern SaaS cards, typography, gradients, lens flare, or high-poly imagery.
Direction A — "A / 1993 TOTAL CONVERSION" printed as a modest board caption. Preserve almost all of the original Doom material language: olive military metal, gritty brown stone, red switches, toxic green slime, dim corridor light, red-orange title letters with metallic beveled depth. AI parody emerges through sprite silhouettes and concise texture details. Enemy: a grinning two-faced demonic mask representing deceptive alignment, rendered like an old Doom imp. First-person weapon is a recognizable chunky pump shotgun subtly modified into a safety-research apparatus. The screenshot should immediately read as classic Doom. Menu and HUD should feel like WAD replacement art from the 1990s, not a contemporary tribute website.
```
